## 1. Official Source Framework & Architectural Principles

### 1.1 Structural Span of the Foundational Stage
Under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and NCF-FS 2022, the Foundational Stage encompasses **5 core years (Ages 3 to 8)**, seamlessly continuous with **Grade 3 (Age 8 to 9)** under the National Mission on Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (NIPUN Bharat).

The official continuum consists of 6 progressive levels:
1. **Pre-School 1 (PS1)**: Age 3–4 (Early Nursery)
2. **Pre-School 2 (PS2)**: Age 4–5 (Lower Kindergarten / LKG)
3. **Pre-School 3 / Balvatika (PS3)**: Age 5–6 (Upper Kindergarten / UKG / Pre-Primary year immediately preceding Class 1)
4. **Class 1 / Grade 1 (G1)**: Age 6–7
5. **Class 2 / Grade 2 (G2)**: Age 7–8
6. **Class 3 / Grade 3 (G3)**: Age 8–9 (Achieving universal foundational mastery)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Developmental Trajectory Principle (NCF-FS 2022, Section 3.2)**:
> Learning Outcomes (LOs) are *developmental trajectories* across an age span rather than rigid, compartmentalized annual silos. Children achieve milestones along a continuum of play, exploration, and practice. The curriculum registry must respect this developmental continuum and not falsely claim that a developmental capability belongs exclusively to a single grade.

NCF-FS 2022 grounds foundational curriculum in the holistic 5-fold development model (*Pancha Kosha*) alongside Positive Learning Habits:

| Domain ID | Official NCF-FS Domain Name | Traditional Concept | Focus Areas | Worksheet Suitability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **SE** | **Socio-Emotional & Ethical Development** | *Manasik / Vaisik Vikas* | Emotional awareness, empathy, relationship building, values & ethical conduct | Emotion recognition: **WORKSHEET_SUITABLE**; Social play: **OBSERVATION_BASED** |

## 3. Curricular Goals (CG-1 to CG-13) and Competencies (C-1.1 to C-13.2)

NCF-FS 2022 defines 13 Curricular Goals across the foundational domains:

### Domain 1: Physical Development (*Sharirik Vikas*)
- **CG-1**: Children develop habits that keep them healthy and safe.
  - **C-1.1**: Shows awareness of personal hygiene, nutrition, and safety practices.
  - **C-1.2**: Identifies nutritious food items and healthy habits. *(Worksheet-suitable: Healthy vs Junk classification)*
- **CG-2**: Children develop sharpness in sensorimotor skills and physical stamina through gross and fine motor abilities.
  - **C-2.1**: Demonstrates gross motor coordination, balance, and body control. *(ACTIVITY_BASED)*
  - **C-2.2**: Demonstrates fine motor control, finger strength, and eye-hand coordination. *(Worksheet-suitable: Pre-writing strokes, tracing, coloring, maze navigation)*

### Domain 2: Socio-Emotional & Ethical Development (*Manasik Vikas*)
- **CG-3**: Children develop a positive self-concept, recognize and manage their emotions, and relate to others with empathy.
  - **C-3.1**: Identifies and names basic emotions (happy, sad, angry, surprised, afraid). *(Worksheet-suitable: Emotion facial matching)*
  - **C-3.2**: Shows awareness of self, family members, and school peers. *(Worksheet-suitable: Family member & helper identification)*
- **CG-4**: Children develop emotional and moral sensibilities, cultivate values, and practice positive social behavior.
  - **C-4.1**: Understands and respects rules, fair play, and caring for the immediate environment. *(TEACHER_LED / OBSERVATION_BASED)*

### Domain 3: Cognitive Development & Environmental Awareness (*Bauddhik Vikas*)
- **CG-5**: Children make sense of the world around them through observation and logical thinking.
  - **C-5.1**: Observes, identifies, and names common objects, animals, birds, plants, and natural phenomena. *(Worksheet-suitable: Living world classification, matching pictures to habitats)*
  - **C-5.2**: Sorts and classifies objects based on one or more observable attributes (color, size, shape, function). *(Worksheet-suitable: Grouping, odd-one-out, categorization)*
  - **C-5.3**: Identifies sequential order of events, daily routines, and cause-effect relationships. *(Worksheet-suitable: Story sequencing, event ordering)*

### Domain 4: Foundational Numeracy & Mathematical Thinking (*Bauddhik Vikas*)
- **CG-6**: Children develop mathematical understanding and abilities to recognize patterns, quantities, numbers, and space.
  - **C-6.1**: Compares quantities using comparative vocabulary (more/less, big/small, tall/short, heavy/light). *(Worksheet-suitable: Quantity comparison)*
  - **C-6.2**: Counts objects with one-to-one correspondence up to 10 (Balvatika), 20 (Grade 1), and counts collections (Grades 2-3). *(Worksheet-suitable: Count and write, object tallying)*
  - **C-6.3**: Recognizes and writes numerals (1–9 in Balvatika; up to 99 in Grade 1; up to 999 in Grade 2; up to 9999 in Grade 3). *(Worksheet-suitable: Numeral identification, number names)*
  - **C-6.4**: Compares and orders numbers using place value, before/after/between, and symbols (`<`, `>`, `=`). *(Worksheet-suitable: Number line, missing numbers, ascending/descending)*
  - **C-6.5**: Understands addition as combining and subtraction as taking away; solves real-world word problems. *(Worksheet-suitable: Visual addition/subtraction, word problems)*
  - **C-6.6**: Performs addition and subtraction operations (1-digit in G1, 2-digit with regrouping in G2, 3-digit in G3). *(Worksheet-suitable: Computation exercises)*
  - **C-6.7**: Understands multiplication as repeated addition and division as equal sharing (Grades 2–3). *(Worksheet-suitable: Array grouping, repeated addition, basic division)*
  - **C-6.8**: Recognizes, extends, and creates patterns with shapes, colors, and numbers. *(Worksheet-suitable: Complete the pattern)*
  - **C-6.9**: Identifies 2D shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle) and 3D shapes, and explores spatial relations (inside/outside, above/below, near/far). *(Worksheet-suitable: Shape sorting, spatial reasoning)*
  - **C-6.10**: Estimates and compares measurement attributes (length, weight, capacity) and understands time/calendar. *(Worksheet-suitable: Clock reading, calendar days, measurement comparison)*
  - **C-6.11**: Recognizes Indian currency (coins and notes) and calculates simple transactions. *(Worksheet-suitable: Coin/note matching, shopping math)*
  - **C-6.12**: Collects, organizes, and interprets simple data using tally marks or pictographs. *(Worksheet-suitable: Pictograph interpretation)*

### Domain 5: Language & Literacy Development (*Bhashik Vikas*)
- **CG-7**: Children develop effective listening and speaking skills for everyday dialogue, comprehension, and expression (L1 / Home Language).
  - **C-7.1**: Listens attentively to simple stories, poems, instructions, and conversations. *(TEACHER_LED / Worksheet: Listening prompt)*
  - **C-7.2**: Expands spoken vocabulary and expresses thoughts, feelings, and questions clearly. *(Worksheet-suitable: Picture description, word meaning)*
- **CG-8**: Children develop emergent literacy abilities to interact with print and literature.
  - **C-8.1**: Shows print awareness, book orientation, and directionality (left-to-right, top-to-bottom). *(Worksheet-suitable: Print trail tracking)*
  - **C-8.2**: Demonstrates phonological and phonemic awareness (rhymes, syllables, beginning and ending sounds). *(Worksheet-suitable: Rhyme matching, initial sound identification)*
- **CG-9**: Children develop decoding skills and read with comprehension.
  - **C-9.1**: Identifies letters/aksharas and associates them with corresponding sounds (*varna-dhwani sambandh*). *(Worksheet-suitable: Letter-sound association, letter circling)*
  - **C-9.2**: Blends sounds to read unfamiliar words, matras, and compound letters (*samyuktakshar*). *(Worksheet-suitable: Word decoding, matra completion)*
  - **C-9.3**: Reads grade-appropriate text with fluency, accuracy, and comprehension. *(Worksheet-suitable: Read and answer, short passage comprehension)*
- **CG-10**: Children develop emergent and early writing skills to express their ideas.
  - **C-10.1**: Uses scribbling, drawing, and inventive spelling to communicate thoughts. *(Worksheet-suitable: Drawing & labeling)*
  - **C-10.2**: Writes letters, words, and short sentences legibly with correct stroke order and spacing. *(Worksheet-suitable: Letter tracing, word writing, fill-in-the-blank, sentence construction)*

### Domain 6: Aesthetic & Cultural Development (*Chaitsik Vikas*)
- **CG-11**: Children develop visual and performing arts abilities and express themselves creatively.
  - **C-11.1**: Explores colors, lines, and textures through coloring, drawing, and craft. *(Worksheet-suitable: Color-by-number, drawing prompts)*
- **CG-12**: Children explore, appreciate, and engage with their natural and cultural heritage.
  - **C-12.1**: Shows awareness of local festivals, folk tales, and community traditions. *(Worksheet-suitable: Community helpers & local environment)*

### Domain 7: Positive Learning Habits (*Pratyahara*)
- **CG-13**: Children develop positive learning habits, curiosity, persistence, and independent thinking.
  - **C-13.1**: Demonstrates sustained attention, curiosity, and completes assigned tasks independently. *(OBSERVATION_BASED)*

## 4. NIPUN Bharat Lakshya (Minimum Learning Benchmarks)

The NIPUN Bharat Mission establishes national *Lakshyas* (targets) for Foundational Literacy and Numeracy:

| Grade Level | Oral Language & Reading Target (Literacy) | Numeracy Target (Mathematics) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Balvatika** (Age 5–6) | Talks about print in books; recognizes letters and sounds; reads at least 2–3 letter simple words | Counts objects up to 10; recognizes and reads numerals 1 to 9; compares collections |
| **Grade 1** (Age 6–7) | Reads age-appropriate unknown text with comprehension at 30–35 words per minute; reads simple sentences | Counts collections up to 20; reads and writes numbers up to 99; performs simple addition and subtraction up to 9 |
| **Grade 2** (Age 7–8) | Reads age-appropriate text with comprehension at 45–60 words per minute; reads with expression | Reads and writes numbers up to 999; adds and subtracts 2-digit numbers up to 99 |
| **Grade 3** (Age 8–9) | Reads age-appropriate text with comprehension at minimum 60 words per minute | Reads and writes numbers up to 9999; adds and subtracts 3-digit numbers; solves simple multiplication and division problems |

## 5. Vidya Pravesh / Balvatika Alignment Matrix

The NCERT *Vidya Pravesh* 3-month school preparation module divides activities into three core developmental goals:
- **Goal 1: Health and Well-being**: Body parts, healthy habits, fine motor coordination (tracing zig-zag, curved, and vertical lines, coloring inside boundaries).
- **Goal 2: Effective Communicators**: Sound discrimination, listening to poems, picture talk, print awareness, tracing letters, letter-sound matching.
- **Goal 3: Involved Learners**: Free exploration of objects, matching pairs, seriation (ordering small to big), pattern continuation, shape recognition (circle, triangle, square), counting objects 1–10.

## 6. Worksheet Suitability vs Activity/Observation Classification

To avoid turning every developmental milestone into inappropriate paper worksheets, the TribeTalk curriculum registry strictly categorizes every competency:

1. **`WORKSHEET_SUITABLE`**:
   - Letter and numeral recognition, sound-symbol matching, phonics decoding, word reading, sentence reading, reading comprehension, vocabulary meaning.
   - Counting objects, number comparison, arithmetic operations, shape sorting, patterns, word problems, money calculations.
   - Pre-writing lines, letter tracing, word copying, fill-in-the-blank, multiple choice questions.
2. **`ACTIVITY_BASED`**:
   - Gross motor skills, running, jumping, balancing, rhythmic movement, sand/clay play, physical games.
3. **`TEACHER_LED`**:
   - Storytelling, open classroom discussions, puppet theatre, conversational turn-taking, shared book reading.
4. **`OBSERVATION_BASED`**:
   - Attention span, task persistence, emotional regulation, empathy towards peers, classroom rule adherence.

## 7. Bilingual TribeTalk Architecture (Hindi + Santali Ol Chiki)

For every worksheet item in TribeTalk, bilingual representations are handled under strict verification rules:
- **Hindi (Devanagari)**: Standard foundational curriculum instruction language.
- **Santali (Ol Chiki - ᱚᱞ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ)**: Verified mother-tongue representation for tribal classroom learning.
- **Verification States**:
  - `VERIFIED`: Confirmed by foundational curriculum database or verified translation memory.
  - `TEACHER_VERIFIED`: Manually input or reviewed by classroom educator.
  - `NEEDS_REVIEW`: Generated draft requiring teacher confirmation before classroom printing.
  - `UNAVAILABLE`: Where authentic Santali equivalent is not yet verified; prompts teacher to provide manual input.

*Zero unverified transliteration into Ol Chiki is permitted.*
